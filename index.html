About

I’m an MBA candidate at Berkeley Haas moving into product management. For over three years at McKinsey, I worked side by side with bankers, sales teams and miners on projects across Central Asia, the Middle East and Africa. I redesigned an SME loan journey that cut time-to-loan from 25 days to 3, rolled out a CRM workflow that tripled weekly corporate sales, and co-built a simple planning tool with a mine section head that helped lift ore output by 20%.

Along the way, I earned a PhD in sociology while working full-time, studying behavior: how trust shapes business partnerships and how households spend. That work was built on 100+ interviews, a year of fieldwork and a 2,700-person survey.

The thread through all of it: products win when they fit how people actually work and decide. Now I want to build them end to end, from the first customer interview to the metric that moves, especially in fintech and AI-powered products.
